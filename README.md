# First Semester

## motion and forces

How can forces on objects be used to predict their motion? How do
different forces affect motion when they interact? How can forces be
used to predict stability? How do engineers use forces and motion to
develop solutions to problems? How do geologic forces affect rock?

## momentum and collisions

How does the mass of an object affect how easily its motion can be
changed? Which quantity do you expect to be conserved when two objects
collide? How can you predict the motion of objects after a collision?
When objects collide, what do you expect to be conserved and what might
not be conserved?

## force at a distance

How does distance affect a force between objects that are not in
contact? What other factors affect forces that act at a distance? How do
objects stay in orbit? How do materials stay together, and why do they
break?

# Second Semester

## energy conversion

How do you know which way energy is going to move? Why does energy
always seem to end up being transferred as heat? How can one type of
energy be transformed into another, more useful type?

## electricity and magnetism

What causes electric current to flow? What causes a magnetic field? How
do electric power plants generate electrical energy? What are the
advantages and disadvantages of different ways to produce electrical
energy? How can electrical energy be produced more sustainably?

## waves

Why are water waves spaced evenly apart? How do we know the structure of
Earth's deep interior, when drills have only penetrated a few kilometers
into the crust? Why can light waves be transmitted across empty space,
while sound waves cannot? What is the difference between analog and
digital?

## nuclear process

Where does the energy emitted by radioactive elements come from? Why are
nuclear explosions so powerful? How do you measure the age of rocks?
